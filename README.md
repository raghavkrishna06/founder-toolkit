# Founder Toolkit
 
Free, practical AI skills to help the founder of a small social-impact organisation make better calls — built from 14 years of working in and around the sector.
 
These aren't abstract frameworks. Each one is a plain-English "skill" you can use inside Claude (or another AI tool) while you're in the middle of the work — deciding whether to shut a programme down, turning a woolly goal into a real one, working out why the same fire keeps starting.
 
---
 
## Who this is for
 
The founder or leader of a small, mission-driven organisation — often under twenty people — who carries every hard decision alone. No COO. Not big enough yet for a board that functions as one. No advisor on speed-dial. If that's you, this toolkit is a structured thinking partner for the moments you're stuck.
 
It's free, and it's meant to be. These are founders who deserve good thinking even when they can't buy it.
 
## What a "skill" is
 
A skill is a folder of instructions an AI tool loads when it's the right moment — it tells the model how to help with one specific kind of problem, step by step. There's no code to run and nothing to configure. Each skill here:
 
- does **one** kind of thing well, and says plainly when your problem isn't one of them;
- asks questions and works through them with you, rather than handing you a verdict;
- leaves you with something written you can act on.
They're built for Claude, but the instructions are plain Markdown, so they port to other tools too (see each skill's README).

## How to use it
 
Open the folder for the skill you want — its README has the exact steps. In short:
 
- **Claude.ai** (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise): download the skill's packaged `.skill` file, rename it to `.zip`, and upload it under **Settings → Features** (code execution must be on).
- **Claude Code**: unzip the skill into `~/.claude/skills/` and it's discovered automatically.
- **Other tools** (Gemini, ChatGPT, etc.): use the skill's combined single-file version as a system prompt or custom instruction.
For the latest, surface-specific steps, see Anthropic's [Agent Skills documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/agent-skills/overview).
 
## The principle behind these
 
Every skill here is built on the same two rules: **be narrow, and be honest about your edges.** A skill tells you plainly what it can't do, keeps the decision and the risk with you, and is stress-tested — including against deliberately difficult cases — before it ships. The aim isn't a tool you depend on. It's a tool that, used a few times, leaves you reasoning better on your own.
